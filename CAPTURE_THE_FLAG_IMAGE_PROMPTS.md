- **One prompt = one image.** Paste one block at a time into ChatGPT.
- Attach your **mode-icons sheet** for #1, and any Chickens vs Turkeys image (e.g. the Land Grab or Egg Toss art) for the rest, so the style matches.
- Ask for the **highest resolution**.
- Upload them with their **Save as** names and say **"Capture the Flag art is ready"**.
