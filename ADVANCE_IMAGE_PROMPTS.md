# ADVANCE — ChatGPT Image Prompts (chess-piece chickens & turkeys)

✔ **Art received and wired in.** All 48 pieces (6 pieces × 4 colours × 2 teams) and the game-card icon are sliced into `assets/sprites/adv_*.webp` and `mode_advance.webp`. The prompts below are kept in case you want to remake any of them.

These prompts make proper **chess pieces that are chickens and turkeys**: 6 pieces × 4 colours for each team (48 pieces), on 4 sheets plus a game-card icon.

## How to use
- **One prompt = one image.** Paste one block at a time into ChatGPT, with your concept sheet attached.
- Flat pure green (#00FF00) background. Claude cuts the pieces out.
- Ask for the **highest resolution**, landscape **1536×1024**.
- Upload them with their **Save as** names and say **"Advance art is ready"**.
- Make the 4 piece sheets in the same chat, one after another, so the style matches.

The 6 pieces are always in this order, left to right: **King · Queen · Rook · Bishop · Knight · Pawn**.

ROW 2 — all 6 pieces in shiny GOLD (gold body and feathers, red comb, orange beak, little sparkles).
