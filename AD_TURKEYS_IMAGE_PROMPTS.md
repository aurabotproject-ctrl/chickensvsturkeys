# TEAM TURKEYS — origin story video (Instagram Reel)
## "Big. Bold. Unstoppable." — storyboard + ChatGPT image prompts

**Format:** vertical 9:16 (1080 × 1920), about **40 seconds**, 30 fps, MP4 with sound effects (add music in Instagram). Same structure as the Team Chickens video, so the two play as a pair.
**Last 5 seconds:** the logo, "JOIN TEAM TURKEYS", "Free for classrooms" and the web address — built from the app's own art, no new image needed.

The chickens are the clever inventors. The turkeys are the opposite kind of hero: **big-hearted guardians and builders**. Everyone thinks they're slow and clumsy, but they're the strongest, bravest protectors on the farm.

## How to use the prompts
- **One prompt = one image.** Paste them one at a time into ChatGPT.
- **Attach 3 references every time:** the **turkey eye close-up** and the **Team Turkeys hero** from the first ad (`ad-05`, `ad-12`) plus the **logo**, so it's clearly the same turkey.
- Ask for **portrait 1024 × 1536**, highest quality.
- **T6 and T9** are on flat green (#00FF00) so they can be cut out and animated. The rest are full scenes.
- Upload them with their **Save as** names and say **"Turkey video images are ready"**.

### Paste this paragraph at the top of every prompt
```
STYLE + CHARACTERS: cinematic, high-end animated-movie look in a bold comic-book style — thick black outlines, rich cel-shaded colour, dramatic rim lighting, subtle halftone texture, family-friendly. The TURKEYS are big, chunky brown turkeys with a huge fanned tail of red, orange, yellow and teal feathers and a RED headband (team colour red). Their leader, GENERAL GOBBLE, is the biggest turkey of all, with an enormous tail, a long-tailed red headband, a proud chest and a warm, confident, fearless grin. The CHICKENS are smaller chunky white chickens with a red comb and a BLUE headband. Match the attached reference characters exactly. Original characters — not based on any existing game, film or brand. No text, no logos, no watermarks.
```
