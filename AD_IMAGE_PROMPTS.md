# CHICKENS vs TURKEYS — 45-second Instagram ad
## "The Golden Egg" — storyboard + ChatGPT image prompts

**Format:** Instagram Reel, vertical **9:16 (1080 × 1920)**, 45 seconds, 30 fps, MP4.
The images below are the cinematic "story" layers. The gameplay shots are recorded from the real app, and the motion graphics (camera moves, shakes, light flashes, kinetic titles in the app's comic fonts, transitions) are built in code. Then everything is rendered to a single MP4.

## How to use the prompts
- **One prompt = one image.** Paste them one at a time into ChatGPT.
- **Always attach these 3 reference images** so the characters stay "ours": the **logo** (`logo.webp`), the **chicken with the cannon** (`c_chicken_aim.webp`) and the **turkey** (`c_turkey_aim.webp` or `turkey_idle.webp`).
- Ask for **portrait 1024 × 1536** (the tallest ChatGPT makes), at the highest quality.
- **Green-screen images (#3, #4–#7, #9, #11, #12)** must be on flat pure green (#00FF00) so they can be cut out and animated in layers. The backgrounds (#1, #2, #8, #10) are full scenes.
- Upload them with their **Save as** names and say **"Ad images are ready"**.

### Already in the app (no need to make)
The **logo**, all the **game icons and sprites**, the comic **fonts** (Bangers, Luckiest Guy), and the **gameplay** itself, which gets recorded straight from the real games.

### Music
Instagram works best with music added inside Instagram (Reels → Add audio), so the ad is made with a **sound-effects track only** (whooshes, thunder, impacts, egg crack, "BOK!" / "GOBBLE!"-style comic hits, timed to the cuts). Pick a trending epic or cinematic track in Instagram on top, or say if you'd rather the MP4 has music baked in (it would need to be a royalty-free track you choose).
