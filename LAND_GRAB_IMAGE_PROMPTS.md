# LAND GRAB — ChatGPT Image Prompts

Land Grab is **already fully playable without any new images**. It uses the existing student avatars as the moving characters (so every player already looks different), a different colour shade for each player (chickens = blues/purples/teals, turkeys = reds/oranges/pinks) and the existing scoreboard art.

These prompts are **optional polish**. Upload them and say **"Land Grab art is ready"**.

## How to use
- One prompt = one image. Attach your concept sheet to the ChatGPT chat as well.
- Sprite sheets use a flat pure green (#00FF00) background. Claude cuts it out.
- Ask for the highest resolution PNG, landscape 1536×1024.

---

## 1. Game card icon (most useful — matches the other game cards)
**Save as:** `mode-paint.png`

```
Create 1 game-mode ICON for a comic-book style classroom game called "Chickens vs Turkeys". Match the attached reference image exactly in style: one chunky cartoon scene, thick black outlines with a thin white outer stroke, cel-shaded flat colour with 2–3 tone shading, subtle halftone dots, saturated punchy colours, light from the top-left, family-friendly.

THE ICON: seen from a high top-down angle, a white chicken in a blue bandana riding a tiny go-kart, zooming out of a chunky blue square patch of land and leaving a curving blue painted trail behind it that loops back towards the patch. In the background a smaller red patch of land with a turkey in a red bandana on its own little go-kart.

Centre the icon in the image with a wide empty green gap (at least 100 px) around it. NO text, letters or numbers.

BACKGROUND: perfectly flat solid pure green (#00FF00), no gradient, no green in the artwork, crisp clean edges.
```

## 2. Banners
**Save as:** `paint-ui.png`

```
Create UI art for a comic-book style classroom game mode called "LAND GRAB" (part of "Chickens vs Turkeys"). Match the attached comic style reference.

STYLE: bold comic-book lettering with thick black outlines, white outer stroke and drop shadow, halftone dots, speed lines, saturated punchy colours, family-friendly. Spelling must be exactly correct.

CONTENTS (separated with generous green space, nothing overlapping):
1. A wide logo: the words "LAND GRAB" in huge slanted yellow-orange comic letters, with a chicken on a go-kart on the left trailing a blue paint stripe, and a turkey on a go-kart on the right trailing a red paint stripe.
2. A starburst banner reading "GRAB LAND!" in green and yellow.
3. A jagged banner reading "SNIP!" in red and white, with a pair of cartoon scissors.
4. A starburst banner reading "OUT!" in red and yellow.

BACKGROUND: perfectly flat solid pure green (#00FF00), no gradient, no green in the artwork, crisp clean edges.
```
