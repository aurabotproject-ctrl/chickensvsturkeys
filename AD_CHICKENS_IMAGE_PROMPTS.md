## How to use the prompts
- **One prompt = one image.** Paste them one at a time into ChatGPT.
- **Attach 3 references every time:** the **chicken eye close-up** and the **Team Chickens hero** from the first ad (`ad-04`, `ad-11`) plus the **logo**, so it's clearly the same chicken.
- Ask for **portrait 1024 × 1536**, highest quality.
- **C6 and C9** are on flat green (#00FF00) so they can be cut out and animated. The rest are full scenes.
- Upload them with their **Save as** names and say **"Chicken video images are ready"**.

### Paste this paragraph at the top of every prompt
```
STYLE + CHARACTERS: cinematic, high-end animated-movie look in a bold comic-book style — thick black outlines, rich cel-shaded colour, dramatic rim lighting, subtle halftone texture, family-friendly. The CHICKENS are chunky white chickens with a red comb and a BLUE headband (team colour blue). Their leader, CAPTAIN CLUCK, is a slightly bigger, brave chicken with a long-tailed blue headband and a confident grin. The TURKEYS are chunky brown turkeys with a big fanned tail of red, orange, yellow and teal feathers and a RED headband. Match the attached reference characters exactly. Original characters — not based on any existing game, film or brand. No text, no logos, no watermarks.
```
